---
question: How does the free trial work?
order: 1
---

The full app is free for 30 days in the app, with no card needed to start. After that, keeping Hubbly needs a subscription. You'll see the price in the app before you choose, and you're never charged unless you subscribe. If you do subscribe, you can cancel any time in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions, or iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
