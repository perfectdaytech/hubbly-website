---
question: Why a subscription?
order: 3
---

Honest answer: Hubbly keeps your family in sync across every device, in real
time, and that means servers running every single day. A small subscription
pays for exactly that, and it means **no ads and no selling your data**,
ever. It also keeps us honest: Hubbly only earns money while it's genuinely
earning its place in your home. The optional one-time packs are cosmetic
treats, never features; everything your family actually uses is in the
subscription.
