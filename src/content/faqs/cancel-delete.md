---
question: How do I cancel or delete everything if I want to?
order: 15
---

You're never locked in. Cancel your subscription any time from the
**Google Play or App Store subscriptions page**; you keep access until the
end of the period you've paid for, and nothing bills you after that. In the
app you can **leave your family** to disconnect a device, **remove old
devices** from Settings, and **delete your whole family's data** in a couple
of taps. After access ends, your data is kept safe for 30 days and then
deleted, or you can delete it immediately yourself.
