## 2. What cookies are

A **cookie** is a small text file that a website can store on your device when you visit. Cookies are often used to remember preferences, keep you signed in, or, most commonly, to track visitors across websites for analytics and advertising.

"Similar technologies" includes things like local storage, pixels and tracking scripts that do a comparable job. Cookies and these technologies are broadly grouped into:

- **Strictly necessary**: needed for the website to load and work, and to keep it secure.
- **Non-essential**: everything else, such as analytics, personalisation and advertising cookies. These normally require your consent.

## 3. Cookies on the Hubbly website (gethubbly.app)

The Hubbly marketing website is a simple, static informational site. We have deliberately built it to be **privacy-friendly**:

- it uses **no analytics or tracking** of any kind: no Google Analytics, no advertising pixels, no behavioural tracking;
- it sets **no non-essential cookies**;
- it does **not** build a profile of you or follow you around the web; and
- there is therefore **no cookie consent banner**, because there is nothing non-essential to consent to.

The only technology that may be involved is whatever is **strictly necessary** for our hosting provider to deliver the site securely and reliably to your browser. This does not identify you and is not used to track you.

## 5. Analytics in the Hubbly app

It is worth being clear about the difference between the **website** and the **app**:

- The **website** (gethubbly.app) uses no analytics and no tracking, as described above.
- The **Hubbly app** does use two services to help us keep it working well: **Sentry** for crash and error diagnostics, and **PostHog** for product analytics (understanding which features are used). These operate **inside the app**: they are not website cookies, and they are not used for advertising. PostHog is configured to avoid collecting personal information that identifies your family members.

How the app handles data, including these two services, is explained in full in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## 6. Managing cookies

Even though this website does not set non-essential cookies, you are always in control of cookies in general. Most browsers let you:

- view the cookies stored on your device;
- block or delete cookies, including those from specific sites; and
- block third-party or cross-site tracking entirely.

Look in your browser's settings under "Privacy" or "Cookies", for example in Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge. Blocking cookies will **not** break the Hubbly website, because it does not rely on non-essential cookies to work.
