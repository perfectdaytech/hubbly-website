---
question: Is my family's data private and safe?
order: 10
---

Privacy is built into how Hubbly works. Hubbly has **no adverts**, never sells your data, and never profiles
children. Data travels encrypted over HTTPS/TLS and is encrypted at rest in
Google Cloud, and a device can only see your family's data once it has joined
with your private code. Read the full detail on our [Safety page](/safety) and
in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
