---
question: What are the optional packs?
order: 5
---

Little one-time treats: looks and themes for the app, quote packs, sound
packs, sticker packs, seasonal looks and a Back to School starter pack. You
buy a pack **once** and it unlocks for your **whole household**. There's
also an **everything bundle** that covers all of them, present and future,
and you can **gift the bundle to another family** with a code. A grown-up
PIN protects all purchases, and no pack ever unlocks a family feature -
those are all in the subscription.
