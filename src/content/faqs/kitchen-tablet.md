---
question: Can I use Hubbly on a kitchen tablet?
order: 12
---

That's one of the lovely ways to use it. Switch on **Kitchen display mode** and
Hubbly keeps the screen awake, locks to landscape, and drifts into a photo
screensaver when the room's quiet, then springs back to the family dashboard
the moment someone walks up. An old iPad or a £40 Fire tablet on the counter
becomes your family's always-on hub. And if you don't have a spare tablet
yet, Hubbly still works beautifully across just your phones.
