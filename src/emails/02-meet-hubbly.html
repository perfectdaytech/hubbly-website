Meet Hubbly

Hubbly is a family organiser for the kitchen tablet and the family phones: routines, calendars, meals, lists and star rewards, in one place.

Coming to Google Play in January, App Store soon after.

30-day free trial in the app, no card needed to start. After that, keeping Hubbly needs a subscription. You'll see the price in the app before you choose, and you're never charged unless you subscribe.
