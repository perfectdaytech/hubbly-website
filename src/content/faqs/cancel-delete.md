---
question: How do I delete everything if I want to?
order: 15
---

You're never locked in. You can **leave your family** to disconnect a device,
**remove old devices** from Settings, and **delete your whole family's data**
in a couple of taps. And because Hubbly Forever is a one-time purchase, there's
no subscription to cancel, and nothing is ever quietly billing you in the
background.
