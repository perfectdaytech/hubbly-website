---
question: How much does Hubbly cost?
order: 2
---

The full app is free for 30 days. The trial runs inside Hubbly, and no card is
needed to start. If the trial ends and you do not subscribe, you will not be
charged. The monthly price on this page is £4.99. An annual plan, if you
choose one, is priced in the store when you subscribe. One subscription covers
the family on every phone and tablet.

**Founders' offer:** A founders' offer may be available on launch day, if the
store listing includes it. Joining the waitlist doesn't reserve a founders'
place. See the [founders' terms](/founders-terms).
