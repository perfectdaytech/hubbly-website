---
question: How much does Hubbly cost?
order: 2
---

The full app is free for 30 days. The trial runs inside Hubbly, and no card is
needed to start. If the trial ends and you do not subscribe, you will not be
charged. After that, keeping Hubbly needs a subscription. You'll see the price
in the app before you choose, and you're never charged unless you subscribe.
One subscription covers the family on every phone and tablet.

**Founders' offer:** Joining the waitlist doesn't reserve a founders' place.
See the [founders' terms](/founders-terms).
