---
question: What devices does Hubbly work on?
order: 7
---

Hubbly is built for **Android and iOS**, on both phones and tablets, and an
Android phone can happily share a family with an iPad. It launches on Android
first, with an iOS version following soon after. It is designed to live on a
kitchen tablet as the family's always-on hub, and to sync to everyone's phones
so you can check the plan or tick something off from anywhere. There is no
special Hubbly tablet to buy: use the screens you already own.
