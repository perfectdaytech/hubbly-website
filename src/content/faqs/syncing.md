---
question: How does syncing across devices work?
order: 12
---

One device creates the family and gets a **6-digit code**. Every other phone
or tablet joins by typing that code, no account, no sign-up forms. From then
on, plan tomorrow on the kitchen tablet, tick chores off on your phone, drop
the after-school pickup into the calendar from anywhere and the kitchen
tablet shows it for the whole family the moment you save. The same family
hub is right there on every joined device.

You can also link a **Google or Apple sign-in as an optional safety net**:
it ties an email address to your family that's only used to recover your
family hub if every device ever gets wiped at once. Apart from that, no
email is required.
