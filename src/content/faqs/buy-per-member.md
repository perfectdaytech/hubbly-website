---
question: Do I have to pay for each family member?
order: 4
---

No. **One subscription covers your whole household.** Parents, kids,
grandparents, carers: everyone joins with a family code. Up to 5 devices
are included, the kitchen tablet plus everyone's phones, and you can swap
an old device out for a new one whenever you like. Big or multi-generation
family? A one-time add-on expands your family to 10 or 15 devices.
