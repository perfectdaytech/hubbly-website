---
question: What if I change my mind? Can I get a refund?
order: 7
---

Subscriptions go through **Google Play or the Apple App Store**, so refunds
are handled by your app store under its refund policy. Your statutory
consumer rights are unaffected.

If your subscription starts after a free trial and you change your mind, you
can cancel within 14 days of the first payment and ask for a refund (less
anything you've used) through Google Play or the App Store. If the store
does not make a refund the law requires, email us and we will look at it.

**30-day free trial, then £4.99 a month.** A payment method is needed. It
**renews automatically** unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the
trial ends.
