---
question: How much does Hubbly cost?
order: 2
---

30-day free trial in the app, no card needed to start. After that, keeping Hubbly needs a subscription. You'll see the price in the app before you choose, and you're never charged unless you subscribe. One subscription covers the whole family on every phone and tablet you join. Your app store shows the price before you confirm anything.

**Founders' offer:** We may offer a lower founders' price when Hubbly launches. If we do, you'll see it on the Google Play page before you subscribe. Joining the waitlist doesn't reserve a place. See the [founders' terms](/founders-terms).
