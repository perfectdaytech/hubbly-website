---
question: How does the free trial work?
order: 1
---

Hubbly is **free to use for 30 days**, with the full app available so you can
try it properly. No payment details are needed to start. There is **no
subscription**: if you decide to keep Hubbly, it's a one-time purchase through
your app store, and the current price is always shown before you confirm. If
it's not for you, nothing happens when the trial ends, and your family's data
stays yours to export.
