## How a referral counts

- You share your own link. We do not collect a friend's email address, and we do not email your friends.
- A referral counts when that person joins through your link and confirms their own email.
- Unconfirmed sign-ups do not count, and we delete them within 21 days.
- Your own sign-ups do not count, including plus-tags and Gmail dot variants. Duplicate or disposable addresses do not count.
- If several sign-ups come from the same internet connection within an hour, we check them by hand before they count.
- A referral stops counting if that person unsubscribes before 23:59 UK time on 31 Dec 2026.
- Referrals close at 23:59 UK time on Thursday 31 December 2026.
- Rewards are digital printables, sent by email by Friday 15 January 2027. They are not in the freebie library.
- [REWARD CHOICE] 3 confirmed referrals: Chore Cards. 5 confirmed referrals: colour-in mascot sheets. The sheets show the mascot only, with no speech bubbles.
- Referrals never give a founders' place or a discount on Hubbly's price. Early testing is not a reward.
- The welcome printable, the Calmer Mornings Starter, is for everyone who confirms. It does not depend on referrals.
- Before the 5-referral reward is sent, we check the referrals by hand.

The free printables are on the [freebie library](/freebies). Referral rewards are not. Promoter: {CONTROLLER_STATEMENT}.
