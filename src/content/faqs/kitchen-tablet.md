---
question: Can I use Hubbly on a kitchen tablet?
order: 13
---

That's one of the lovely ways to use it. Set a tablet up as your **kitchen
display** and Hubbly keeps the screen awake, drifts into a photo screensaver
when the room's quiet, and springs back to the family dashboard the moment
someone taps. Big type and bright colours make it readable from across the
kitchen. Any Android tablet or iPad on the counter can become the family
hub, and if you don't have a spare one, Hubbly works beautifully across
just your phones.
