---
question: Do my children need their own accounts?
order: 8
---

No, and that's deliberate. Hubbly has **no child logins** at all. A grown-up
sets up one family, and children simply use the shared hub. Their routines,
stars and pages are surfaces a parent creates and controls. Kids get a space
that feels like theirs without ever handing over an email address or a
password.
