---
question: What if I change my mind? Can I get a refund?
order: 7
---

Subscriptions and packs go through **Google Play or the Apple App Store**,
so refunds are handled by your app store under its refund policy: request
one from your store's order history. Your statutory consumer rights are
unaffected, and we honour any refund the law requires. Cancelling is always
free and instant from your store's subscriptions page, and the trial is a
full 30 days with every feature unlocked, so you can be completely sure
before you spend a penny.
