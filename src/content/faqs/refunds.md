question: What if I change my mind? Can I get a refund?
order: 7
---

Subscriptions go through **Google Play or the Apple App Store**, so refunds
are handled by your app store under its refund policy. Your statutory
consumer rights are unaffected.

If you subscribe and change your mind, you can cancel within 14 days of your
first payment and ask for a refund (less anything you've used) through Google
Play or the App Store. If the store does not make a refund the law requires,
email [hello@gethubbly.app](mailto:hello@gethubbly.app) and we will look at it.

30-day free trial in the app, no card needed to start. After that, keeping Hubbly needs a subscription. You'll see the price in the app before you choose, and you're never charged unless you subscribe.
