---
question: What happens to our data if we cancel or stop?
order: 6
---

Nothing dramatic, and nothing sneaky. You keep full access until the end of
the period you've paid for (or the end of your trial). After that, your
family's data is **kept safe for 30 days** in case you come back, and then
deleted. If you'd rather not wait, you can **delete everything immediately**
in the app, any time.
