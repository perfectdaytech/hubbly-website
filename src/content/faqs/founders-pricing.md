---
question: 'What is "Founders pricing"?'
order: 5
---

Hubbly is launching with a limited offer for early households: a lower one-time
price, as a thank-you for backing Hubbly early. This launch pricing is limited
and may rise as Hubbly grows, so the current price is always shown before you
buy. Any later change applies only to new customers, never to a purchase you
have already made.
